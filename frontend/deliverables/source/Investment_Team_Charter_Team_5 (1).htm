Team Mission Statement:

Our mission is to act as a professional investment group/team that makes informed decisions using research and data, manages risk carefully, and applies course concepts to a real-world investing simulation.
Our practical objective is to outperform a simple 60/40 benchmark over the simulation period while avoiding large drawdowns that would be unacceptable for a real client.

Team Norms, Accountability & Conflict Resolution

Define expectations for professionalism, participation, and conflict resolution.

Expectations for preparation and participation:

●       Communicate early with the team if any issues arise (via Teams)

●       Actively contribute to the discussions

●       Complete the task assigned on time

How will missed deadlines or non-performance be handled?

●       First miss: Team Lead checks in privately (life happens or soft extension)

●       Second miss: Formal team discussion; workload reallocation if needed

●       Persistent non-participation: Escalate further (last resort; prefer internal resolution)

Conflict resolution process:

●       Direct first: Members in conflict discuss 1-on-1 within 48 hours

●       Mediation: If unresolved, the Team Lead facilitates group discussion

●       Further escalation: Only if conflict threatens team function or violates academic integrity
